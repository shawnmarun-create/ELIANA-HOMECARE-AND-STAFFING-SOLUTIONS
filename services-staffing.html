Staffing roles we provide

Qualified professionals ready to support your facility or organization.

Registered Nurse (RN)

Comprehensive nursing care, patient assessments, care planning, medication administration, and clinical leadership. Our RNs are experienced in acute care, long‑term care, palliative care, and emergency settings.

Acute Care LTC Palliative Emergency

Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN)

Professional nursing care including medication administration, wound care, patient monitoring, and healthcare support. Our LPNs work alongside RNs and physicians to deliver quality patient outcomes.

Medication Admin Wound Care Patient Monitoring

Personal Support Worker (PSW)

Compassionate personal care and daily living support, promoting dignity and independence. Our PSWs assist with bathing, dressing, grooming, mobility, and meaningful engagement.

Personal Care Mobility Companionship

Health Care Aide (HCA)

Support with mobility, personal care, and daily healthcare needs in a safe, respectful environment. HCAs work under the supervision of licensed nurses to ensure continuity of care.

Mobility Support Personal Care Vital Signs

Caregiver / Companion

Companionship, emotional support, supervision, and assistance with daily activities. Perfect for clients who need social engagement and light support rather than clinical care.

Companionship Supervision Daily Activities

Live‑in Caregiver

Full‑time in‑home support, companionship, and assistance with daily living activities. Ideal for clients who require ongoing presence and support throughout the day and night.

24/7 Presence Full-time Support Room & Board

Disability Support Worker

Support for individuals with disabilities through personal care, life skills training, and community engagement. We focus on independence, dignity, and quality of life.

Life Skills Community Access Personal Care

Mental Health Support Worker

Emotional support, supervision, and assistance for individuals experiencing mental health challenges. Our workers are trained in de‑escalation, crisis prevention, and therapeutic communication.

Crisis Prevention Emotional Support Therapeutic Communication

Rehabilitation Support Worker

Assisting clients during recovery and rehabilitation — wellness, mobility, and daily living activities. Ideal for post‑surgery, injury recovery, and transitional care.

Post‑Surgery Injury Recovery Mobility Training

Travel Nurse (RN/LPN)

Flexible, short‑term nursing assignments across Alberta — competitive pay and travel support. Perfect for facilities facing seasonal surges, staff vacancies, or special projects.
