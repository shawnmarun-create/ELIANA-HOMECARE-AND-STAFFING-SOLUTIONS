Founder's Story

A house built on compassion.

Eliana Home Care & Staffing Solutions was founded from a place of compassion, dedication, and real-life healthcare experience.

As a healthcare worker with hands-on experience in caregiving and support services, I have witnessed firsthand the importance of dignity, patience, and compassionate care. Working closely with individuals and families has taught me that caregiving is more than a profession — it is a calling rooted in empathy, trust, and human connection.

Through my experience in healthcare settings, I recognized the growing need for reliable, compassionate, and professional support services for individuals who require care and assistance. This inspired the vision behind Eliana Home Care & Staffing Solutions — a company committed to delivering quality care while treating every client with respect, dignity, and kindness.

At Eliana, we believe every individual deserves personalized care, meaningful support, and a safe environment where they feel valued and respected. Our mission is to provide dependable home care and healthcare staffing solutions that positively impact the lives of clients, families, and communities.

We are committed to professionalism, compassion, integrity, and excellence in everything we do.

Fortunate Mugwara CEO & Lead Care Coordinator
