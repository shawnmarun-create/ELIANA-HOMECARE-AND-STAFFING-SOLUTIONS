About the role

We are seeking Registered Nurses (RN) and Licensed Practical Nurses (LPN) for travel contracts across Alberta and surrounding provinces. What you'll do

Patient assessments & care planning
Medication administration
Wound care & IV therapy
Collaboration with healthcare team
Patient & family education

  • ✓ Strong clinical assessment and adaptability
