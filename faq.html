Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers from families and healthcare professionals. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us →

How do I know Eliana offers a high quality of care?

We are CARF accredited, ISO-certified, and every caregiver undergoes rigorous screening (criminal record check, reference checks, competency verification). We also provide ongoing training and 24/7 supervision. Our clients rate us 4.9/5 stars from over 380 families served.

Do my caregivers have the skills for my health condition?

Yes. We match caregivers based on specific needs — dementia, post-op, palliative, complex care. Our care coordinators brief caregivers on each client's file before assignment. We specialize in Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, stroke recovery, and many other conditions.

How are your services different from hiring a private caregiver?

We offer comprehensive care management: planning, scheduling, coordination, and supervision. We take the effort out of logistics, giving you peace of mind and a single point of contact. You also get backup coverage when your regular caregiver is unavailable.

What are the benefits of aging at home?

Most seniors prefer to age in place — in familiar surroundings with cherished memories. Our services allow them to do so safely, with personalized care, technology-enhanced safety (LifeCare Plus), and coordination with doctors and hospitals. Benefits include reduced stress, better mental health, and maintaining independence.

How quickly can care start?

We can typically start care within 24-48 hours of your free in-home assessment. For urgent needs, we offer emergency response rates with same-day placement. Call our 24/7 care line to discuss immediate needs.

Is there a contract or minimum commitment?

No long-term contracts. We offer flexible arrangements that can change as your needs change. Some services have a 3-hour minimum visit, but you're never locked into an extended commitment.

Why should I apply to be a caregiver with Eliana?

Flexible schedules, competitive pay ($25-$110/hr depending on role), paid training, 24/7 support, and real career growth opportunities. We treat you as a valued team member, not a number. Plus, you'll have access to health benefits, retirement planning, and daily pay options.

What training do I need?

We hire caregivers with a range of experience. If you have a caring personality, we can train you on specific caregiving skills. Specialized training for dementia, palliative care, medication administration, and more is provided at no cost to you.

Are caregivers at Eliana employees or contractors?

All of our caregivers are employees. This means we provide benefits, handle payroll, withhold taxes, and cover workers compensation insurance. You're protected, supported, and valued as a team member.

Do I need a car to be hired?

Owning a vehicle is not required, but a valid driver's license and clean driving record are needed to drive clients' cars for errands and appointments. Some roles may require your own vehicle — we'll discuss during the interview.

What type of schedule can I expect?

Flexible schedules: full-time, part-time, live-in, or as-needed. You choose what works for you — mornings, evenings, weekends, or overnights. We'll work with your availability, not against it.

What support do caregivers receive?

24/7 on-call support from our office team, ongoing training and CEU credits, access to a family portal for updates, and regular check-ins from your care coordinator. You're never alone — we're always just a phone call away.

Still have questions?

Our Care Managers are available 24/7 to answer any questions you may have.
