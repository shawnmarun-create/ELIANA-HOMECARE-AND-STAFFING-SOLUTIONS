Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining our team.

Why should I apply to be a caregiver with Eliana?

We offer flexible schedules, competitive pay, paid training, and a supportive team culture. You'll have 24/7 support and real career growth opportunities. Plus, you'll be part of a team that genuinely values your contributions.

What training do I need?

We hire caregivers with a range of experience. If you have a caring personality, we can train you on specific caregiving skills. Specialized training for dementia, palliative care, and more is provided at no cost to you.

Are caregivers employees or contractors?

All of our caregivers are employees. This allows us to provide benefits, handle payroll, withhold taxes, and cover workers compensation insurance. You're protected, supported, and valued.

Do I need a car?

Owning a vehicle is not required, but a valid driver's license and clean driving record are needed to drive clients' cars for errands and appointments. Some roles may require your own vehicle.

What type of schedule can I expect?

Flexible schedules: full-time, part-time, live-in, or as-needed. You choose what works for you. We'll work with your availability, not against it.

How do I apply?

Click the "View Open Positions" button above, browse available roles, and submit your application online. Our hiring team will reach out within 48 hours.
