Who We Are

Compassionate care,
professional staffing.

Eliana Home Care & Staffing provides warm, professional in-home care for seniors and families across Calgary, and dependable nursing & PSW staffing for the facilities that serve them.

Every family we serve and every caregiver we place carries that promise forward — with compassion, care and trust.

Called to Care.
For the people who raised us.

At Eliana, we believe that aging at home should be a choice rooted in dignity, not necessity. Too many families face confusing systems, rushed care, and heartbreaking decisions. We set out to change that.

Our team of skilled professionals works alongside you — listening first, acting second. Whether it's a few hours of companionship each week or round‑the‑clock complex care, every Care Plan is as unique as the person receiving it.

For facilities, we provide vetted, credentialed staff who arrive ready to work. No gaps. No guessing. Just reliable healthcare professionals who share our commitment to excellence.

What we believe

Our values guide every decision, every interaction, every day.

Compassion First

We believe that every person deserves to be treated with the same love, patience, and respect we would give our own family members. Compassion isn't a department — it's the foundation of everything we do.

Excellence Always

We hold ourselves to the highest professional standards — rigorous screening, continuous training, and ongoing supervision. Because your loved ones deserve nothing less than the best care available.

Trust & Transparency

No hidden fees. No surprise bills. No confusing contracts. We believe in honest communication, clear pricing, and building relationships that last — because trust is earned, not assumed.

Our commitment to you

When you choose Eliana, you're not just getting a caregiver or a staffing agency. You're gaining a partner who genuinely cares about your family's well‑being and your facility's success.

  • Same‑day response — We answer calls 24/7, because needs don't wait for business hours.
  • Continuity of care — We match you with consistent caregivers who build genuine relationships.
  • Full transparency — You'll always know who is coming to your home, when, and why.
  • Advocacy & support — Our Care Managers navigate the healthcare system alongside you.
